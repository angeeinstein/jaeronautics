These membership terms and conditions govern joining online and the technical administration of membership in **joanneum Aeronautics – Luftfahrtinnovationsverein an der FH JOANNEUM**, as well as the use of the members' portal, the members' forum and the association's internal teams.

Short name: **joanneum Aeronautics**  
Alte Poststraße 149  
8020 Graz  
Austria

ZVR number (Austrian register of associations): **345540358**

Email: **office@joanneum-aeronautics.at**

## 2. Relationship to the association's rules

The association's statutes and rules of procedure, as amended from time to time, apply to membership in addition to these terms. Individual teams may also have their own team rules.

These membership terms govern in particular the practical handling via the members' portal, electronic communication, fees, payments and team functions.

Mandatory statutory rights remain unaffected.

## 3. Joining online

Membership can be applied for via `members.joanneum-aeronautics.at`.

The information marked as mandatory in the form must be complete and correct. The private email address also serves as the login and must be verified.

Before the joining process is completed, the documents relevant to the membership are made available electronically.

The membership is activated according to the process provided in the portal as soon as the required information and confirmations have been given and – where a fee is due – the required payment has been completed successfully.

The association reserves the right not to accept a membership in justified cases or to end it in accordance with the association's rules.

## 4. Member categories

The members' portal records in particular the following categories:

- Student;
- Alumni;
- Staff / Lecturer;
- Company / Partner;
- Honorary member.

For students, a year group or cohort may also be recorded.

These portal categories serve administrative purposes. Voting rights, the right to stand for election and the classification under the law of associations are governed by the statutes in force.

## 5. User account

The member account is personal.

Login details must not be passed on without authorisation. Members must take reasonable measures to prevent third parties from accessing their account.

In the event of a justified suspicion of misuse or a security problem, access may be suspended temporarily.

Suspending a user account is not automatically the same as an exclusion from the association.

## 6. Keeping data up to date

Members must keep the contact and master data required for administering the association up to date.

Certain contact details can be changed directly in the portal.

Changes in particular to name, member category or year group can be made via a change request and are approved or rejected after review by an authorised person.

## 7. Membership fee

The regular membership fee is currently **EUR 15 per calendar year**.

The regular fee period runs from **1 January to 31 December**.

The specific amount due is shown in the portal before the joining process is completed.

## 8. Joining during the year

When joining during the calendar year, in principle only the share attributable to the remaining period is charged for the first year.

When joining **from 1 October**, no membership fee is currently charged for the period up to 31 December. The first regular annual payment is made with the following renewal.

## 9. Payment processing

Membership and team fees are processed via **Stripe**.

Depending on the payment methods available, payment can be made in particular by payment card or SEPA direct debit.

Card and bank account data are not stored in the members' portal but processed on the payment pages provided by Stripe.

## 10. Automatic renewal

Unless it has been cancelled for the end of the current fee period, the membership is renewed for a further calendar year on **1 January** of each year.

The membership fee due for the new calendar year is charged via the payment method set up with Stripe.

Stripe may send corresponding payment or renewal information before an upcoming renewal.

## 11. Changes to the membership fee

Changes to the regular membership fee apply at the earliest to a following renewal period.

Members concerned are informed by email of a change to the fee applicable to the next renewal at least **14 days before the renewal at the changed amount**.

The member can cancel the membership before the renewal and thereby prevent a renewal at the changed fee.

## 12. Failed renewal payment

If a renewal payment cannot be collected successfully, the membership initially remains active for a **grace period of 21 days**.

During this time, further payment attempts or payment notifications may take place.

If the payment is not completed successfully within the grace period, the membership ends.

A later new membership is possible by paying again or joining again.

## 13. Cancellation

The membership can be cancelled via the member account at any time for the end of the current calendar year.

The cancellation prevents the next automatic renewal. The membership in principle continues until the end of the fee period already paid for or under way.

In principle there is no entitlement to a pro-rata refund for a fee period already under way, unless mandatory statutory rights or an express decision of the association provide otherwise.

Statutory withdrawal and consumer rights expressly remain unaffected.

## 14. Rights of withdrawal when joining online

Insofar as mandatory statutory provisions on distance selling or withdrawal apply to joining online, they are not restricted by the above cancellation and refund rules.

In particular, a statutory right to revoke or withdraw from a contract concluded online within a statutory withdrawal period remains unaffected.

## 15. Account deletion

A member can request the deletion of their portal account via the designated process confirmed by email.

When an account is deleted, running Stripe subscriptions are ended. An account deletion does not in itself give rise to a claim to a refund of fees already paid, unless mandatory statutory rights provide otherwise.

Payment and accounting data that must be kept by law may continue to be retained despite the account deletion.

The data protection details are set out in the privacy policy.

## 16. Chargebacks and reversed payments

If a payment is disputed and the chargeback is finally decided against the association, the amount concerned is deemed not to have been paid.

Insofar as no paid membership period remains as a result, the membership may be ended accordingly.

## 17. Members' forum

Members may receive access to the internal Discourse forum.

Forum access is administered via the members' portal. The portal may in particular transmit the forum username, name, profile picture, year group and team and group memberships to the forum.

Using the forum requires the corresponding access authorisation.

Additional rules of conduct for the forum may be laid down separately.

## 18. Teams

Membership in the association and membership in an internal team are administered separately in the portal.

Membership in the association does not automatically lead to membership in a particular team.

Depending on the team, an application, invitation or other approval may be required.

## 19. Team applications

A team application may in particular require an application text and further team-related information.

The responsible team leads decide on admission within the framework of the association and team rules provided for.

## 20. Team rules

A team may use its own rules or team regulations.

Insofar as team rules are relevant for joining, they are made available electronically before joining the team or during the application process.

The portal may document when and in which version the respective team rules were accepted.

Changes to team rules may be recorded in the portal with the old and new version.

Material changes are announced to the members concerned in an appropriate manner. Where renewed consent is required, it is obtained separately.

## 21. Team fees

A team may

- be free of charge;
- provide for a recurring team fee; or
- charge a one-time fee per fee period.

The amount, fee period and payment method are shown before any payment is made.

## 22. Joining a team late

When joining during a team fee period already under way, the **full team fee intended for this period** is currently charged in principle.

A pro-rata reduction only applies if this is expressly provided for by the respective team.

## 23. Recurring team fees

If a recurring fee is provided for a team, it is renewed or collected via Stripe at the start of each next team fee period.

Where enabled, Stripe may send a corresponding reminder before the next charge.

## 24. One-time team fees per period

For team fees paid separately for each period, there is no automatic renewal.

The portal may send a reminder of the next payment about **14 days before the end of the period paid for**.

If the fee is paid in time, the team membership can continue without interruption. Otherwise, the team membership that depends on the fee ends on the last day of the period paid for.

## 25. Leaving a team voluntarily

When leaving a team voluntarily, the team membership in principle runs until the end of the period already paid for.

Team fees already paid are in principle not refunded pro rata, unless mandatory statutory rights or an express arrangement to the contrary exist.

## 26. Ending a team membership

A team membership can be ended within the framework of the applicable team and association rules.

Running team subscriptions are ended in particular if

- a team membership is ended by an authorised team lead;
- the association membership ends; or
- the member account is deleted.

In the case of early termination initiated by the association or the team, mandatory statutory claims to repayment or other rights remain unaffected.

## 27. Rejoining a team

If a team membership ended solely because a team fee was not renewed, a simplified rejoining by payment, without a new application, may be made possible within **six months**.

For other reasons for ending, a new application or approval process may be required.

## 28. Use of team fees

Team fees collected via the portal are allocated to the respective team in full.

The fees for payment processing via Stripe are currently borne by the association and are not deducted from the contribution allocated to the team.

The internal use or payout of team funds is governed by the association's internal finance and approval rules.

## 29. Team roles

Special roles may be assigned for teams, in particular team lead and team treasurer.

These roles only carry the portal permissions required for the respective task.

Member data may only be used within the scope of the association or team task assigned.

## 30. Room and infrastructure access

Insofar as access to rooms or infrastructure must be organised for a team, the member data required for this may be transmitted to the competent bodies.

The data protection details are described in the privacy policy.

## 31. Electronic communication

Membership-related communication generally takes place electronically to the email address stored in the portal.

This includes in particular:

- email verification;
- password reset;
- information on the membership;
- fee and renewal information;
- information on profile changes;
- team applications and team memberships;
- payment information;
- information and invitations required under the law of associations, insofar as electronic delivery is provided for.

Members must provide an email address at which they can be reached and enter changes promptly.

## 32. Data protection

Information on the processing of personal data can be found in the association's separate privacy policy.

Agreeing to these membership terms is **not a general consent under data protection law**.

Required consents are obtained separately where necessary.

## 33. Termination for other reasons

Exclusion from the association and other reasons for termination under the law of associations are governed by the association's rules in force.

Suspending portal access or ending a team membership is not automatically the same as an exclusion from the association.

If the association membership ends, portal, forum and team access that depends on it may be ended.

## 34. Liability and mandatory rights

The statutory provisions on liability apply.

Mandatory consumer and membership rights are not restricted by these terms.

## 35. Changes to these terms

These membership terms bear a version date and may be adapted for the future if, in particular,

- legal or regulatory requirements;
- the technical design of the members' portal;
- payment or communication service providers;
- the association's internal administrative processes;
- the member or team functions offered; or
- other material circumstances

change, or if a clarification or addition to the terms is required.

Changes are not applied retroactively to matters already fully completed merely because a new version has been published.

### 35.1 Changes during a running membership

Changes that are material for existing members are communicated in an appropriate form in good time before they take effect, in particular by email or via the members' portal.

A mere notification does not entitle the association to arbitrarily worsen material obligations or to introduce new payment obligations during a membership period already paid for.

Insofar as a change for a running membership requires the express consent of the member, it is obtained separately.

### 35.2 Changes for a following fee period

Conditions for a future renewal or fee period may be changed with effect for that future period. Members are informed of material changes before the start or renewal of the period concerned.

The member can end an upcoming automatic renewal in good time in accordance with these terms. For changes to the membership fee, the special information rule in section 11 also applies.

### 35.3 No deemed consent

Silence or the mere continued use of the portal does not automatically count as consent to a change, unless such deemed consent is legally permissible and has been effectively agreed in the specific case.

### 35.4 Relationship to statutes, rules of procedure and team rules

Changes to the statutes, the rules of procedure and team rules are governed by the internal resolution and amendment rules of the association provided for this purpose.

Insofar as an effectively adopted change to a higher-ranking set of association rules affects these membership terms, the terms are adapted accordingly and members are informed of material effects.

## 36. Applicable law and internal dispute resolution

Austrian law applies, insofar as a choice of law is permissible and mandatory consumer protection provisions are not thereby restricted.

For disputes within the association, the dispute resolution mechanisms provided for in the statutes also apply.

Mandatory statutory places of jurisdiction and rights remain unaffected.
