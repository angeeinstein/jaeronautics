This privacy policy applies to the online services operated by **joanneum Aeronautics – Luftfahrtinnovationsverein an der FH JOANNEUM**, in particular

- the public website `www.joanneum-aeronautics.at`, including the webshop, events, contact and support functions; and
- the members' portal `members.joanneum-aeronautics.at`, including membership administration, membership and team fees, team administration and the connection to the members' forum.

The two services are operated separately on a technical level but have the same controller under data protection law. The information is therefore combined in a single privacy policy.

## 2. General legal bases

We process personal data only insofar as this is necessary for operating our online services, administering memberships, handling enquiries, processing orders and events, administering teams, processing payments or complying with legal obligations.

Depending on the processing, we rely in particular on:

- **Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR**, where the processing is necessary for the performance of a contract or a membership relationship, or for steps taken prior to entering into one;
- **Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR**, where the processing is necessary for compliance with a legal obligation;
- **Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR**, where the processing is necessary for the purposes of legitimate interests and these are not overridden by the interests of the data subject;
- **Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR**, where we ask for consent.

Where special categories of personal data are exceptionally processed, this is done only on a legal basis permitted for this purpose under Art. 9 GDPR.

# Part A – Public website, webshop and events

## 3. Odoo Online / provision of the website

The public website is operated with **Odoo Online**.

The service provider is in particular:

**Odoo S.A.**  
Chaussée de Namur 40  
1367 Grand-Rosière  
Belgium

When the website is accessed, technically necessary data is processed. This may include in particular the IP address, date and time of access, requested URL, referrer, browser and device information, and technical log and security data.

The processing serves to provide the website, manage sessions, analyse errors and keep the system secure. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; where a specific function is provided to perform a contract or at the user's request, additionally Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Odoo generally processes the personal data stored in our Odoo instance as a service provider for the association and may in turn use sub-processors for hosting, infrastructure and security.

## 4. Cloudflare

We use **Cloudflare** for DNS, domain infrastructure and individual redirects or protected endpoints.

In doing so, in particular the IP address, requested hostname, timestamps and technical connection and security information may be processed.

The main website `www.joanneum-aeronautics.at` is currently served directly by Odoo. Cloudflare is therefore not to be understood as a CDN for all content of the main website.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest lies in the secure and reliable operation of our internet services.

## 5. Cookies and browser storage on the public website

The public website uses cookies and comparable browser storage technologies.

We use the cookie bar built into Odoo. Visitors can choose between necessary and additional optional cookies or services. The choice made is stored.

The following have been observed in particular:

- `session_id` for the session, login, shopping cart and security functions;
- `frontend_lang` for the language setting;
- `tz` for the time zone;
- `website_cookies_bar` for the cookie choice;
- in the shop, where applicable, entries such as `seen_product_id_*` for product or interaction history.

Odoo also uses local storage for technical and functional states, among other things for presence, live chat and push functions.

Technically necessary storage access is used insofar as it is required for the requested function, session management or security. Optional cookies and optional tracking services recognised by Odoo are controlled via the cookie bar.

## 6. Odoo / Plausible Analytics

On the public website we use the **Plausible Analytics function** built into Odoo Online.

In doing so, in particular page views, URLs visited, referring pages and technical browser and device information may be processed. The Odoo Plausible integration we have observed does not use conventional Google Analytics cookies.

We use the analysis to understand the use of the website in aggregated form and to improve the website technically and in its content.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest lies in a data-minimising analysis of reach and usage.

## 7. Google Fonts

The public website loads fonts, in particular **Roboto** and **Poppins**, via Google Fonts.

In doing so, the browser connects to Google's servers. In particular, the IP address and usual technical HTTP connection data are transmitted to Google.

The integration serves a consistent and technically reliable presentation of the website. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

With the current Odoo configuration, Google Fonts are loaded even if only necessary cookies have been selected in the cookie bar.

## 8. Browser push notifications / Firebase

Odoo loads technical components of **Google Firebase**, in particular Firebase Cloud Messaging, to provide browser push notifications technically.

Merely providing these libraries does not automatically register a browser for push notifications. Actually signing up requires a corresponding decision by the user or browser permission.

If push notifications are enabled, in particular technical device or browser identifiers, registration tokens and connection data may be processed.

The technical provision is based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. A voluntary sign-up for push notifications is based on Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR.

## 9. Contact form

If you contact us via the contact form, we process in particular the information you enter, such as:

- name;
- email address;
- phone number;
- company;
- topic;
- subject and message;
- attachments provided voluntarily.

The processing serves to handle your enquiry and the necessary follow-up communication.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR insofar as the enquiry relates to a contract or pre-contractual measures, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

## 10. Helpdesk and support

Various support and helpdesk areas are offered via the public website.

Depending on the form, in particular name, email address, phone number, company, username, problem category, subject, description and attachments may be processed.

The processing serves to handle and document the support request. The legal basis is, depending on the context, Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR.

## 11. User account on the public website

The Odoo website allows a user account to be created and used.

In doing so, in particular name, email address or login, authentication and security data, session data, password reset information and, where applicable, technical passkey/WebAuthn data may be processed.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR; security-related processing is additionally based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

## 12. Webshop, shopping cart and wish list

When using the webshop, in particular products and variants viewed, shopping cart contents, wish lists, search and filter entries and, where applicable, product or interaction history may be processed.

The processing serves to provide the shop functions and to prepare or carry out orders.

The webshop currently offers **pick-up** only. Shop items are currently not shipped.

## 13. Orders

For orders, we process in particular:

- name;
- contact and, where applicable, billing/address data;
- products, variants and quantities ordered;
- price;
- order number;
- payment status and transaction references;
- pick-up details.

The processing is carried out for order and payment processing, invoicing, organising pick-up, handling complaints and complying with legal obligations.

The legal bases are Art. 6(1)(b) and, where applicable, (c) GDPR.

## 14. Stripe

We use **Stripe** for online payments on the public website and in the members' portal.

For European Stripe accounts, the processing is carried out in particular by **Stripe Payments Europe, Limited**.

In the course of a payment, in particular name, email address, billing data, amount, currency, order or membership references, payment method, IP address and technical device information and, depending on the payment method, card, bank or other payment data may be processed.

Card and bank data are processed directly by Stripe wherever possible. We receive in particular the payment status and transaction references in return.

The legal basis for payment processing is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. Statutory, accounting and security-related processing may additionally be based on Art. 6(1)(c) or (f) GDPR.

Stripe may carry out certain processing, for example for fraud prevention, risk analysis and its own legal obligations, as a controller in its own right.

## 15. Back-in-stock notification

If you sign up for a back-in-stock notification for a product, we process your email address and the product concerned for this purpose.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. Once the purpose has been fulfilled, the data is deleted unless there is another legal basis for further storage.

## 16. Event registrations

Registrations for events may be offered via the website.

Depending on the event, we process in particular:

- name and contact details;
- participation options;
- information on accompanying persons;
- organisational preferences, for example meal options;
- payment information for events subject to a fee;
- other information required or provided voluntarily for the specific event.

The specific mandatory and voluntary information is shown in the respective form.

The processing is carried out to organise, hold and settle the event. The legal basis is generally Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR; for non-contractual association events, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR may apply.

If special categories of personal data are exceptionally required, for example health-related information on allergies or necessary arrangements, we provide separate information in the respective form and, where applicable, obtain explicit consent.

# Part B – Members' portal

## 17. Purpose and hosting

The members' portal at `members.joanneum-aeronautics.at` serves in particular for

- registering and administering association memberships;
- administering personal member accounts;
- collecting and administering membership and team fees;
- administering internal teams and team memberships;
- the connection to the members' forum;
- supporting internal access and organisational processes of the association.

The portal is operated on a server of the association in **Microsoft Azure, region West Europe (Netherlands)**.

Cloudflare is also used for domain and connection infrastructure.

The legal basis for the technical operation is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; insofar as the processing is necessary for the membership and the services connected with it, additionally Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

## 18. Registration and portal user account

For registration and account administration, in particular the following is processed:

- private email address as login;
- password, stored only as a password hash;
- date of the email verification;
- roles and permissions, such as admin or treasurer roles;
- where applicable, account suspension status and reason;
- registration date and the documented acceptance of the terms applicable at the time of joining.

The processing serves to set up, secure and administer the account and the membership.

## 19. Member profile

The members' portal processes in particular:

- salutation and title;
- first name and last name;
- street and house number;
- postal code, town and country;
- private phone number;
- private email address;
- optional work or university phone number and email address;
- member category;
- year group or cohort, where applicable.

A university email address provided can be verified via a confirmation link, in particular to be able to confirm a stated student status.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

## 20. Membership and fee data

The portal stores in particular:

- start and end of the membership;
- renewal date;
- membership status;
- scheduled cancellation at the end of the period;
- paid membership periods;
- Stripe invoice and transaction references;
- Stripe customer, subscription and checkout IDs;
- team fees, including the amount, the period paid for and, where applicable, the amount refunded;
- where the association offers a credit balance: the balance and every change of it -- top-ups, cash handed over, what was paid with it (what and from whom, the association or a team), refunds and corrections -- with date, amount and, for bookings made by hand, who made them.

The credit balance is shown to the member and to the administrators and the association's treasurer. Remaining credit is refunded when the member account is deleted.

**Card or bank account data is not stored in the members' portal.** It is processed on the payment pages provided by Stripe.

The legal bases are Art. 6(1)(b) and, insofar as statutory retention obligations exist, Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR.

## 21. Profile changes

Certain master data such as name, member category and year group can only be changed via a change request.

In particular the request, the administrative decision and, where applicable, a message to the member are stored.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR; the traceable administrative documentation may additionally be based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

## 22. Members' forum / Discourse

The members' forum is operated as a separate **Discourse** system and connected to the members' portal via single sign-on.

For forum access, permissions and profiles, in particular the following data may be transmitted to the forum:

- email address;
- forum username;
- full name;
- profile picture;
- year group/cohort;
- group and team memberships;
- where applicable, administrator or moderator status.

Name and profile picture may be visible to other forum members within the members' forum.

In the members' portal, the profile picture is shown to the member themselves, to the other members and the leads of the teams they belong to, and to administrators. For accounts taken over from the former forum, the former forum's profile picture is used for this until a new one has been uploaded and approved.

An uploaded profile picture is stored in the portal and may be reviewed by an administrator before it is approved. An approved profile picture may be retrievable without login from the portal server under a URL that cannot easily be guessed, so that the forum can technically retrieve the file.

For accounts taken over from the former forum, the old username, display name, profile picture and year group may additionally be stored in order to reassign existing accounts and archived posts and to show the profile picture as described above.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR and, for permission and security management, additionally Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

## 23. Teams, applications and memberships

The portal supports internal teams.

In doing so, in particular the following may be processed:

- applications and application texts;
- team memberships;
- start and end of the team membership;
- reason for ending it;
- team roles such as team lead or team treasurer;
- messages when leaving a team;
- team fees.

The processing is carried out to organise the teams, handle applications, manage permissions and process team fees.

## 24. Team rules

Teams may use their own team rules.

The portal may store:

- whether team rules were accepted;
- the date of acceptance;
- the version in force at the time of acceptance;
- changes to team rules, including the old and new version.

This serves the traceable organisation of team memberships.

## 25. Internal notes by team leads

Team leads may create internal, factual notes on applicants or team members.

These notes are not normally visible in the members' area, but they remain personal data. They are therefore also included in a personal data export of the data subject.

The processing is based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest lies in traceable and orderly team administration. Such notes may only be used for factual association and team purposes.

## 26. Team bank details

For teams, the account holder, IBAN and BIC may be stored. The account holder may also be a private individual.

Changes may be recorded in the audit log with the old and new value.

The processing serves to handle and traceably administer team funds.

## 27. Data access by team leads, treasurers and administrators

Team leads only receive the information intended for organising the team. This may include in particular:

- name;
- profile picture;
- private and university email address;
- phone number;
- year group/cohort;
- member since;
- paid until;
- application information;
- team-related internal notes.

This information may be provided in the portal view and partly as a CSV export.

Team treasurers and the association's treasurer receive the information required for administering fees and payments.

Administrators receive the access required for the technical and administrative management of the association.

Access may only take place within the scope of the respective function in the association.

## 28. Room access lists

Where a team needs access lists for the use of rooms or infrastructure, the names and verified university email addresses of current team members may be transmitted to the competent bodies, for example the responsible staff of FH JOANNEUM.

The portal may store the list last transmitted in order to be able to track changes in later transmissions.

The legal basis is, depending on the arrangement, Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR.

## 29. Audit log

The members' portal keeps an audit log. In particular the following may be recorded in it:

- who carried out a relevant action;
- the type and time of the action;
- logins;
- administrative actions;
- payments and status changes;
- for changes, where applicable, the previous and new values.

According to the current technical state, the audit log stores **no IP addresses**.

The processing serves traceability, security, error analysis and the proper administration of the association and its finances.

At present, no fixed automatic deletion period is configured for the audit log. When an account is deleted, personal before/after values are removed as provided for.

## 30. Email and notification logs

The portal stores information about transactional emails sent or scheduled, in particular the recipient, message type, delivery status and technical errors.

According to the current implementation, these logs are kept for **one year** and then deleted automatically.

## 31. Sending of emails by the members' portal

Portal emails are sent via configurable SMTP services.

According to the current technical state, in particular **IONOS** and **Brevo** are used, depending on the sender. In doing so, in particular the recipient address, name and the content of the respective email are transmitted to the sending service used.

The association's general email infrastructure outside these portal transactional emails is operated via **Microsoft 365 / Exchange Online**.

## 32. Stripe webhooks

The portal processes technical Stripe webhook events. In doing so, in particular event IDs are stored so that the same payment event is not processed more than once.

The processing serves the correct synchronisation of payments, subscriptions and membership status.

## 33. Cookie in the members' portal

According to the current state, the members' portal uses **one technically necessary session cookie**.

This cookie

- keeps the signed-in session going;
- supports the protection of forms;
- is configured as `Secure`, `HttpOnly` and `SameSite=Lax`;
- is currently valid for up to **seven days**.

The members' portal currently uses no web analytics of its own, no advertising and no tracking pixels. Fonts, scripts and stylesheets are delivered by the portal itself.

A consent banner for optional tracking purposes is therefore currently not required for the portal itself.

Stripe's separate payment page may use its own cookies and storage mechanisms.

## 34. Web server and security logs

The web server of the members' portal may keep technical access logs. These may contain in particular the IP address, timestamp, requested resource and technical status information.

According to the current system default, the nginx logs are typically kept for about **14 days** as part of log rotation.

Short-lived rate-limit counters serve to protect against automated login attempts. The email address used for this is hashed.

## 35. Backups

Encrypted backups are created for data protection purposes.

According to the current technical state:

- the last five regular backups are kept on the server;
- they are encrypted with AES-256;
- the passphrase is not stored together with the backup;
- additional database copies are created before software updates, of which only the most recent are kept.

The processing serves the recoverability and security of the system.

# Part C – Common provisions

In doing so, in particular the email address, name, sender and recipient data, subject, message content, attachments and delivery information may be processed.

The public website contains links to external services, in particular Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, TikTok and Google Maps.

In the current technical arrangement, these are generally plain links and not embedded social media feeds or maps.

A direct connection to the respective external service is therefore generally only established when the link in question is opened.

- Odoo S.A. and its sub-processors;
- Microsoft in connection with Azure and Microsoft 365;
- Cloudflare;
- Stripe Payments Europe, Limited and the necessary banks and payment networks;
- Google in connection with Google Fonts and Firebase on the public website;

## 39. Transfers to third countries

Some of the service providers used belong to internationally active groups of companies and may also process data outside the European Economic Area.

Where data is transferred to countries without an adequacy decision, a transfer mechanism permitted under the GDPR is used, in particular appropriate contractual safeguards, where required.

## 40. Retention period

We store personal data only for as long as this is necessary for the respective purpose or statutory retention obligations exist.

For the public website, the periods depend in particular on the respective process. Contact and support data is kept only for as long as necessary for handling and appropriate documentation. Event data is stored for as long as necessary for organisation and follow-up. Order, invoice and accounting data is stored in accordance with the statutory retention obligations.

For the members' portal, the following currently applies in particular:

| Data category | Rule |
|---|---|
| Account and member profile | During the membership; thereafter currently until deleted by the data subject or an administrator. |
| Registrations never paid for | Deleted 90 days after registration or the last payment attempt; email warning beforehand. |
| Payment records | Generally 7 years, insofar as necessary as evidence for tax and accounting purposes. |
| Stripe data | In accordance with Stripe's own statutory and contractual retention obligations. |
| Audit log | Currently without a fixed automatic deletion period; personal change values are removed when an account is deleted, as provided for. |
| Portal email/notification logs | 1 year. |
| Internal team notes | Deleted with the member account. |
| Regular encrypted backups | The last 5. |
| Update database copies | Only the most recent copies. |
| nginx web server logs | Typically about 14 days. |
| Login rate limits | Short-term. |

Data may also be stored for as long as this is necessary for the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims.

## 41. Deletion of a member account

Members can request the deletion of their portal account via the designated process confirmed by email.

According to the current technical state, in particular:

- running Stripe subscriptions are ended;
- personal profile and account data is deleted or overwritten;
- forum links and profile pictures are removed;
- internal team notes are deleted;
- personal information in the portal's email history is removed;
- remaining credit is refunded to the payment method it was paid with; credit handed over in cash is paid out by the association;
- payment records that must be kept by law, including the history of the credit balance, are retained without any direct personal identification that is no longer required.

Data retained by Stripe on the basis of its own legal obligations is not automatically deleted as a result.

## 42. Obligation to provide data

Providing personal data is generally voluntary. However, certain information is required if a specific function is to be used, for example membership, payment, team application, order, event registration or support.

Without this required information, the respective service may not be able to be provided.

## 43. Automated decisions

We do not ourselves make any decisions based solely on automated processing that produce legal effects concerning a person or similarly significantly affect them.

Stripe may carry out automated risk and fraud checks in the course of its own statutory and security-related tasks.

## 44. Rights of data subjects

Subject to the statutory requirements, there are in particular the rights to:

- access;
- rectification;
- erasure;
- restriction of processing;
- data portability;
- object to certain processing based on legitimate interests;
- withdraw consent given, with effect for the future.

Requests can be sent to **office@joanneum-aeronautics.at**.

## 46. Changes to this privacy policy

This privacy policy is information about our data processing as it stands and not a separate contract with the data subjects.

We can and must therefore adapt it if, in particular, our online services, technical systems, service providers used, recipients, purposes of processing, retention rules or the legal requirements change.

The current version is provided centrally on the public website. The members' portal may link to this central version. In the event of material changes that are particularly relevant for existing members or users, we inform them in an appropriate manner, for example via the members' portal, by email or by a clearly visible notice on the website.

Where consent is required by law for new or changed processing, it is obtained separately. A mere change to this privacy policy does not replace a required consent.

The date given at the beginning of this privacy policy indicates the status of the respective published version.
