## § 1 Scope

i) The Flight Team Ravens is a team of the association "joanneum Aeronautics". These team rules apply in addition to the association's statutes and rules of procedure.

ii) Detailed team rules will follow. Until then, the following principles apply.

## § 2 Principles

By joining the team, every member agrees

i) to treat other members and third parties with respect and fairness;

ii) to follow the rules of the team and of the association, and the instructions of the team leads, especially on safety;

iii) to take care of the team's rooms, equipment and material;

iv) not to harm the team or the association, whether through their conduct or by damaging their reputation.

## § 3 Breaches

Anyone who breaches these principles may be excluded from the team. The association's statutes remain unaffected.
